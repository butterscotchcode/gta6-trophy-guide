A complete guide and tracker for all 55 GTA VI trophies on PlayStation 5 — offline in your browser. Your progress never leaves your machine.

## What it does

- **All 55 trophies, exact official data** — every name, description and grade from the official list (verified against Exophase on 19 Nov 2026; PSNProfiles agrees on every name, description and grade), with Exophase rarity as of launch day.
- **A guide in our own words for every trophy** — what to do, in what order, and what can be missed. Where a place or trigger is not yet pinned down (the game is a day old), the guide says so instead of guessing.
- **Tracker** — tick trophies off, with progress counters for every trophy PSN tracks a count for (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles, 50 challenges, 52 areas, 9 gangs, $250,000 laundered, and more). Progress is saved in your browser.
- **Filters and search** — by kind (story, side strands, activities, collectibles, exploration, combat, money), by grade, by text, and hide-earned.
- **Spoiler setting** — story trophies stay hidden (name and description) until you earn them.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** — seven stages in the order that wastes the least time, each with its own progress bar.
- **Missables** — the possibly-missable trophies and the watch list, built from the official descriptions, with the uncertainty stated plainly.
- **Next up** — a planner that suggests the next five trophies to chase and shows which counted trophies are closest to done.
- **Export / import** — back up your progress as a JSON file or move it to another browser. Per-trophy notes, saved alongside.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts        # then open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network: the app is static files under `app/` and loads `app/data/trophies.json` from the same folder.
